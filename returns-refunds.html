Returns & Refunds

Handmade with care — here for you if something isn't perfect.

Last updated: September 2026

Every ÆTHERRA piece is handcrafted in small batches, and many are one of a kind. Because we cannot resell skincare items or handmade goods that have left our studio, we do not accept returns or issue refunds. That said, your satisfaction matters deeply to us — if your order arrives defective, we will make it right by fixing or replacing the item, free of charge.

1. No Returns or Refunds

All sales are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or offer refunds for:

  • Changes of mind.
  • Skincare products that have been opened or used — for health and hygiene reasons.
  • Normal wear of handmade goods, including the natural patina that copper develops over time.

2. Defective Items — We Fix or Replace

If your item arrives defective (for example, a broken wire wrap, cracked bead, or damaged product), we will repair or replace it free of charge, including return shipping. Here is how it works:

  • Contact us within 14 days of delivery at aetherraholistic@proton.me or through our contact page.
  • Include your order details and a photo of the issue so we can assess it quickly.
  • We will repair your piece, or replace it — if your item was one of a kind, we will craft a comparable piece in its place.

Defective claims must be reported within 14 days of delivery to be eligible. We will never leave you with a piece that is not whole and beautiful.

3. What Is Not Covered

  • Damage caused by normal wear, accidents, or improper care.
  • Natural changes such as copper patina, tarnishing, or minor variations in handcrafted pieces.
  • Skincare items that have been opened or used, even if the product did not suit your skin.

Because our skincare is plant-based and formulated without harsh preservatives, please always perform a patch test before first use and check the ingredients for allergens.

4. Damaged or Lost in Transit

If your order arrives damaged during shipping, or never arrives within the expected delivery window, please contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date. We will repair, replace, or reship your order at no cost to you. Please review our Shipping Policy for delivery expectations.

5. Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy, or an issue with your order, please reach out at aetherraholistic@proton.me. We are a small studio and we answer every message.
