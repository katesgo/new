Shipping Policy

How your order is prepared and delivered.

Last updated: September 2026

Every ÆTHERRA piece is handcrafted with care in Toronto, Canada. This policy explains how we prepare and ship your order, what to expect along the way, and what to do if something goes wrong with delivery.

1. Processing Time

Most orders are ready to ship within 2 – 5 business days. A few things to note:

  • Made-to-order items are crafted after your order is placed and may take up to 7 – 10 business days before shipping.
  • One-of-a-kind and ready-to-ship items leave sooner, usually within 2 – 3 business days.
  • We are a small, independent studio — processing times may occasionally run longer during busy seasons, and we thank you for your patience.

2. Shipping Destinations

We currently ship within Canada only. Every order is lovingly packed and sent from our studio in Toronto, Ontario, using our trusted national carrier.

3. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

  • Standard shipping: flat rate of $10.00, delivered within approximately 3 – 7 business days.

Free standard shipping on all orders over $75 (before taxes). Delivery to remote or rural areas may take a few extra days. Estimated delivery times begin after your order ships and depend on the carrier and destination. They are estimates, not guaranteed.

4. Order Confirmation & Tracking

Once your order is placed, you will receive an email confirmation. When your order ships, we will send you a second email with a tracking number so you can follow your parcel's journey.

5. Taxes

All orders are shipped within Canada, so there are no customs duties or import fees. Any applicable taxes are calculated at checkout.

6. Damaged or Lost in Transit

Please inspect your parcel as soon as it arrives. If your order arrives damaged, or never arrives within the expected delivery window, reach out to us at aetherraholistic@proton.me within 14 days of the expected delivery date, and we will make it right. See our Returns & Refunds policy for details.

7. Incorrect Shipping Address

Please double-check that the shipping address you provide is complete and correct, including any unit or apartment number. If you need to change an address after ordering, contact us as soon as possible. We cannot be held responsible for parcels sent to an address provided at checkout, and reshipment costs may apply if an incorrect address causes a returned parcel.

8. Questions?

If you have any questions about shipping, please reach out through our contact page or email us at aetherraholistic@proton.me.
