Solfeggio

The ancient frequencies & the crystals that sing with them

The Solfeggio scale is a set of six (and later eleven) sacred tones said to carry the original harmonic language of creation. Each frequency was used in ancient chants to console, inspire, and heal. Crystals are not the sound itself — but they are the still, resonant body that remembers it. Stone and tone share the same geometry; when you wear a crystal tuned to a frequency, you carry that vibration with you through the day.

Below, each Solfeggio frequency meets the stones that echo its intention. Stones marked "Shop" are in our current collection.
